## Personal Impressions

It was a pity that several pieces were missing, but the overall quality of the performance was still very good. Yu Zibei sang three songs and was remarkably steady on the second day. The singer of Qingyi's EP was also excellent.

Compared with FES, the audience may have been a little less intense, but the energy was still impressive. Everyone joined in with the familiar jokes, and the atmosphere was great.
